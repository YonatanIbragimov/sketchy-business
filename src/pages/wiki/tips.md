## General

- **Collect the shavings.** Every level has some; the count is in the bottom-left corner. The Narrator says they'll
  matter. He's right, for once.
- **Every flag saves.** A little "Saved" appears in the corner. Pause → **Save & quit** whenever you need to stop.
  **Continue** on the title menu picks up at your last flag.
- **Pick any chapter you've reached** on the title screen with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>.
- **Assist mode** (Settings → Gameplay) gives you 5 hearts and a slightly slower, more forgiving game. Switch it on or
  off any time.
- **Listen to the Narrator.** Between the insults he's usually telling you how the next bit works.
- **Not sure where to go?** Stand still for a few seconds and a red arrow points the way on.
- **There's a flag after every puzzle room**, so a slip on the jumps after it doesn't mean solving it again.
- **A chapter's goal counts from the foot of its landmark** (the Great Sharpener's pencil arch, for one): no need to walk into the machine itself.

## Minions

- **Nub** (eraser) takes two hits. Stomping counts.
- **Springclip** (paper clip) hops at you. One hit.
- **Tack** hangs under shelves and drops point-first when you walk underneath. Don't dawdle under shelves.
- **Smudgelings** shiver, then swoop. Move the moment they shiver.
- **Snips** (scissors) shake, then charge. Jump when they shake, then hit them from behind.

## Movement

- **Dodge** (roll with no direction held) makes hits pass straight through you. It's the best defensive move in
  the game.
- **Dash in the air** to stretch a jump, then double jump at the end of it.
- You **can't climb one wall** by kicking off it again and again. Look for a second wall or a ledge.
- **Saw blades:** jump the low ones, slide under the high ones. Do not hug them.

## For the curious

- Clean Slate Keep has a Hall of Portraits. Every single picture is of Lord Smudge. He's very consistent.
- Somebody drew a horse with five legs in the Paper Mountains. We don't talk about the horse.
- Want to speedrun it? Settings → Gameplay → **Speedrun timer**.
