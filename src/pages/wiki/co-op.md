The whole game can be played by two people on one screen. Player 2 is **Penny**, a ballpoint pen from across the
gutter. She has her own lines and her own story running alongside Noggin's.

## Starting co-op

On the title menu, set **Players: 2**. Already playing? Pause and choose **Penny joins (co-op)**. The level
starts over with her in it.

| Controllers plugged in | Noggin | Penny |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Controller 1 (and the keyboard) | Controller 2 |
| 1 | Keyboard | The controller |
| 0 | Left side of the keyboard | Right side of the keyboard ([keys](/wiki/controls#two-players)) |

Either player's Start pauses the game.

## How it plays

- **One screen, one camera.** It zooms out to keep you both in frame. You can't get more than a screen apart.
- **Falling.** Fall and you're redrawn next to your friend after a moment. If you're both down, it's back to the
  checkpoint. Penny's Back button redraws her next to Noggin.
- **Heads.** You can stand on each other's heads. Jump off a head for the **team jump**, which goes much higher.
- **Bosses** have half as much health again and pick on one of you at a time (marked **!**). Hit a boss while it's
  busy with the other player for a **blindside**: double damage, and it turns on you.
- **Ink can't be erased.** Smudge's eraser just shoves Penny along.

## Two-player puzzles

Some rooms only appear in co-op:

- **Hold the door:** one of you stands on the switch, the other gets through.
- **Twin buttons:** both pressed at the same time.
- **Team jump:** a button too high for one doodle.
- **Heavy plate:** needs both of you standing on it.
