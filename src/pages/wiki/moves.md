Noggin's body took about three seconds to draw, but it does a surprising amount. Buttons are on the
[controls](/wiki/controls) page.

## Getting about

- **Jump.** Hold the button for a higher jump, tap for a hop. You get a split second after running off a ledge to
  still jump, and a jump pressed just before landing still counts.
- **Double jump.** Jump again in mid-air, with a flip. ("He jumped off absolutely nothing. Physics has filed a
  complaint.")
- **Wall slide and wall jump.** Hold toward a wall while falling to slide down it, then jump away. You can't climb a
  single wall by kicking off it over and over: you need two walls, or a ledge.
- **Ledge grab.** Jump at the edge of a platform and Noggin hangs on. Press up to climb, down to let go.
- **Slide.** Press down while running to slide under low gaps (and high saw blades).
- **Crouch and squat-walk.** Hold down while standing to crouch, then move to shuffle along in a deep squat. It's a
  bit slower than walking, and you can stop and turn round whenever you like. Slide or roll into a gap too low to
  stand in and Noggin stays down and squat-walks until there's room overhead. You can't jump until you can stand up.

- **Roll.** A quick roll in the direction you're going.
- **Dash.** A burst of speed on the ground or in the air, then a 1.5 second cooldown.
- **Drop through.** Down + Jump on a thin plank drops you through it.

## Fighting

- **Headbutt** (chapters 1 to 3). Same button as the swing, with a short lunge. It only hurts a mini-boss while the boss
  is stunned.
- **Swing the Last Pencil** (from chapter 4).
- **Stomp.** Land on a minion's head. Works the whole game.
- **Dodge.** Press roll with no direction held: Noggin ducks and steps back, and hits pass straight through him. Great
  against lance thrusts and charges.
- **Hearts.** You have 3 (5 in assist mode). Fall or run out and you're back at the last flag.

## Getting about, the fancy way

| Thing | How it works |
|---|---|
| Springs | Walk onto them. "Springs are honest like that." |
| Swing rings | Jump to grab, let go at the top of the swing. |
| Zip pulls | Grab and zip down the wire. Let go before the end. Or don't, and find out. |
| Paper planes | Ride them across the sky in chapter 5. |
| Moving platforms, lifts, ferries | "It goes where it goes. Get on, ride, get off. Riveting." |
| Paper ledges | They tear a moment after you land. Keep moving. |
| Conveyor belts | Some run with you. Most, being machines, don't. |
| Sticky coffee | You crawl through it and jump like a wet sock. |
| Gusts | Wind pushes you sideways; use it to cross wide gaps. |
