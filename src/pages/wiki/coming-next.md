## Six new chapters, six protectors

Lord Smudge doesn't fight fair. Before the final showdown he hides behind **six protectors**, each guarding a piece of
something the Artist once drew: the recipe for an **Ultimate Pencil**, one that can draw anything at all. Smudge can't
be beaten with the plain old Last Pencil. So Noggin goes and takes the pieces back, one protector at a time.

Every new chapter is a new place with its own scenery, four levels, and an **element that's bad news for a world made
of drawings**: it changes how you move and fight across the whole chapter, not just in the boss fight.

## Out now: the Crayon Kiln

The Kiln is where the Artist's broken crayons go to be melted down, and the floor is **molten wax**. Paper planks
start smouldering the moment you stand on them, embers rain down (watch for the glowing mark where one is about to
land), and little ember sprites flare before they dash at you.

**Wick** is waiting at the bottom. He floats out of reach over a pool of wax, so you fight him from **chunks of
cooled wax that sink while you stand on them**: keep moving. He lobs embers that scorch the chunk they land on, rolls
smoke rings along the floor, melts the chunk out from under you, and, once he's angry, snuffs himself out and drops
on you from above. He only comes close enough to hit when he stops to relight.

## The Ultimate Pencil

Each protector you beat gives you one piece. You'll see the pencil fill in as you go. Once it's whole, the last fight
with Smudge changes completely: he erases the world, and you **draw** it back. The shavings you've been collecting
all game finally get a proper job too.

- New achievements for every new chapter.
- New errand characters with their own stories in every level.
